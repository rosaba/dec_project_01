# Project plan for project-01-group-05

## Objective

A short statement about the objective of your project.

    The objective is to uncover patterns and risk factors in NYC motor vehicle collisions with data driven insights. 

## Consumers

What users would find your data useful? How do they want to access the data?

    Emergency services, City Council & road engineers and tourists may find the data useful. ES may find the data useful to evaluate traffic safety intiatives. City Council & road engineers may find the data useful to design safer streets and intersections. Tourists may find it useful to know when to travel to NYC. 

## Questions

What questions are you trying to answer with your data? How will your data support your users?

    What are the most common contributing factors to collisions in NYC?
    Which borough has the highest number of collisions?
    What time of day do most collisions occur?
    How do collision rates vary by day of the week?
    What are the most dangerous intersections in NYC?
    Are collisions increasing or decreasing in specific boroughs? 
    What types of vehicles are most often involved in collisions? 
    Fatality rates by vehicle type and cause?
